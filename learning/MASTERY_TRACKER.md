Remove the example row after adding the first real topic.

## Status rules

- **New:** Encountered but not yet explained reliably.
- **Practicing:** Partial understanding with remaining gaps.
- **Can Explain:** Clear and accurate teach-back without major help.
- **Can Apply:** Independent and correct application in a realistic task.

Do not raise a status based only on confidence. Use evidence.
